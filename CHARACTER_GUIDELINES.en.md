# Character Usage Guidelines for "Suzu"

Last updated: November 21, 2026
Rights holder: Lis-louis

> This is an English translation. If it differs from the [Japanese version](CHARACTER_GUIDELINES.md), the Japanese version takes precedence.

The program (source code) in this repository is published under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
However, **the character "Suzu" (鈴) is not covered by the MIT License.**
Please follow these guidelines when using Suzu's design or images.

---

## 1. Scope

The following are called "Suzu assets" in these guidelines:

- The name, appearance, and design of the character "Suzu" (鈴), including her outfit, hairstyle, hair ornament, colors, and expressions
- The images in `Resources/Sprites/`
- The icon images in `Resources/AppIcon/` and `docs/images/`
- The character sheets in `鈴の画像です。これを作って欲しいです/`
- Any images made from the above (including cropped, resized, or edited versions)

All copyrights and other rights to Suzu assets belong to Lis-louis.

## 2. What you may do

As long as you follow the conditions (Section 3) and the prohibitions (Section 4), you may freely do the following without contacting us:

- Use this app (the official version) and spend time with Suzu on your desktop
- Share screenshots or videos showing Suzu on your personal social media (not monetized)
- Draw and share fan art of Suzu (non-commercial)
- Fork this repository on GitHub to contribute to development
- Modify this app for use only on your own computer

## 3. Conditions

### Credit (required)

When you share Suzu or publish fan art, please include the following credit:

```
キャラクター「鈴」© Lis-louis
https://github.com/Lis-louis/suzu-desktop-focus
```

You may also write the credit in English:

```
Character "Suzu" © Lis-louis
https://github.com/Lis-louis/suzu-desktop-focus
```

Where a URL is hard to include, the credit line alone is fine.

### About fan art

- Please respect Suzu's design.
- Make sure it is not mistaken for official work (we recommend labeling it as "fan art").

## 4. Prohibited uses

### 4-1. Uses that go against public decency

The following are prohibited, including in fan art:

- Sexual content, or content that emphasizes exposure
- Excessively violent or cruel content
- Discrimination, hate speech, defamation, or content that hurts others
- Promoting or recruiting for any political, religious, or ideological cause
- Illegal acts, or content that encourages illegal acts
- Content that seriously damages the image of Suzu or the rights holder

### 4-2. Reposting and redistribution

- Extracting Suzu's images and reposting them to other websites, social media, asset collections, etc.
- Redistributing Suzu's images on their own
- Claiming Suzu or her assets as your own creation

### 4-3. Commercial use

Making a profit using Suzu is prohibited. For example:

- Selling goods, illustrations, or data
- Using Suzu in monetized streams or videos (including when she merely appears on screen)
- Using Suzu in paid apps, services, or content
- Using Suzu in advertising or promotion

### 4-4. AI training and generation

- Using Suzu's images or design as training data for AI (image-generation AI, LoRA, fine-tuning, etc.)
- Using AI to create images resembling Suzu and publishing or distributing them

### 4-5. NFTs and similar

- Turning Suzu assets into NFTs or anything similar

## 5. Distributing a modified version of the app

The app's program is under the MIT License, so you may modify and distribute it.
However, a modified version is **not the official Suzu**, so before distributing it you must:

- Remove all Suzu assets (everything listed in Section 1)
- Not use the name "Suzu" or "鈴", or her design, in the app's name, icon, description, etc.
- Not present it in any way that could be mistaken for the official version

Please put images of a different character of your own in the modified version (see [docs/ASSETS.md](docs/ASSETS.md) for how to add assets).

## 6. Other

- If you want to use Suzu in a way not covered here, or are unsure whether your use is allowed,
  please ask first via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Lis-louis/suzu-desktop-focus/issues).
  Depending on the case, we may grant individual permission.
- If Suzu is used in violation of these guidelines, the rights holder may request that it be taken down or deleted.
- These guidelines may change without notice. The updated guidelines apply from the time they change.
