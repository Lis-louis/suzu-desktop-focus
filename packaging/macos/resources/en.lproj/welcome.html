Welcome to Suzu

This installs Suzu (version @VERSION@), an app where the original character Suzu lives on your Mac's desktop.

  • She walks around, stands on top of windows, dozes off, and reacts to clicks
  • Drag to move her, double-click to open the chat window, right-click for the menu
  • Open the Control Panel (start, stop, settings) from the Dock icon

Suzu.app will be installed in your Applications folder.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
No Screen Recording or Accessibility permission is needed (only window positions and sizes are read).
